# Notchlight 1.0.2

Choose whether Notchlight shows Codex usage **used** or **remaining**.

## New

- In Settings, enable **Connect to Codex**, then choose **Usage display → Remaining**.
- The border length, preview, settings percentage, hover details, and notch menu
  follow your choice for the selected five-hour or weekly window.
- The choice persists between launches. **Used** remains the default, and
  Reset appearance preserves your usage-display preference.
- Missing data stays unavailable; refresh failures retain the last known value.
  Manual border ranges are preserved when disconnecting from Codex.

## Download

Download `Notchlight-1.0.2-macOS-arm64.zip`, unzip it, and move `Notchlight.app`
to Applications. Verify the archive with the accompanying `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

This build is **Apple Silicon only**, **ad-hoc signed**, and **not notarized**.
If macOS blocks the first launch and you trust the download, follow
[Apple's Open Anyway instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).

Requires macOS 14 or later. Built and tested on macOS 27 with Xcode 27;
macOS 14/26 and Intel hardware have not been runtime-tested.

## Verification

All **125 automated tests passed**, including display persistence, window
selection, empty/full/fractional usage, missing data, stale hover details,
and preservation of manual border settings. Release packaging, the extracted
app's signature, bundled legal documents, deployment target, and archive
checksum were verified.

The native Settings fixture was checked in light and dark appearances, at
500×480 minimum content size, and while scrolling. The picker, immediate
percentage/preview updates, and keyboard menu selection passed. Screenshots
use sample data. VoiceOver speech and physical-notch hover were not re-tested.

## Notes

Usage normally refreshes once per minute, and local activity approximately
every two seconds. These are indicators; use Codex itself as the authority
for account limits and task state. Codex features require your own installation
and sign-in. Local activity and optional swipe monitoring depend on implementation
details that may change with future Codex or macOS releases.
