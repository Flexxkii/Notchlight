## What the app does

Notchlight draws a configurable border around supported MacBook notches. You
can optionally connect it to your existing Codex installation to display account
usage and local task activity. It is an independent utility, not an official
Apple or OpenAI product, and neither company endorses it.

You need your own compatible Mac and, for the optional integration, your own
Codex installation and sign-in. Notchlight does not include a Codex subscription,
additional usage, or a promise that OpenAI will provide access to any service.
Your relationship with those providers remains governed by their terms.

## Usage readings and compatibility

Usage readings can be delayed, unavailable, or incomplete. Account usage normally
refreshes once per minute; local activity is checked approximately every two
seconds. These are informational indicators, not billing statements or a
guarantee that a task has finished. Check Codex itself before relying on a limit,
reset time, or task state.

Local Codex file formats and macOS behavior can change. Optional swipe detection
uses undocumented event fields and may stop working after an OS update. The
application is supplied without a promise of compatibility with future systems,
continuous availability, maintenance, or support. Nothing here removes mandatory
legal rights or obligations.
